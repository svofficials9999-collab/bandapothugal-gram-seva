## New card source ledger

### Vidya Bharosa - listed proposal, rollout unverified

Peddapalli's Yuva Vikasam page lists a Vidya Bharosa student-card proposal. That brief page does not establish current implementation orders,payments or deadlines; it also does not prove non-implementation. Confirm current Education Department/government orders. Not an application card.

Official source: https://peddapalli.telangana.gov.in/scheme/yuva-vikasam/

### Agricultural electricity - category-specific subsidy

The official 2026-27 tariff lists Rs 0.00 energy charge for non-corporate farmers under LT-V(A), subject to DSM conditions. Corporate agriculture, nurseries and other categories differ. Not all connections/charges are free. Confirm your category and other charges with the electricity office; separate from domestic Gruha Jyothi.

Official source: https://tgsouthernpower.org/resources/PDF/Tariffs/69tarifffile.pdf

### Rythu Bima - enrolled farmers

The state page describes enrolment for eligible farmers aged 18-59, government-paid LIC premium and Rs 5 lakh nominee cover on death of an enrolled farmer due to any cause. Verify land/enrolment/nominee details, current policy and claim documents with the Agriculture Extension Officer. No automatic enrolment or payment-date guarantee.

Official source: https://rythubandhu.telangana.gov.in/Default_LIC1.aspx

### National Livestock Mission (NLM)

The central page lists poultry, sheep, goat and pig entrepreneurship/breed improvement; feed/fodder; and research, insurance and extension sub-missions. Terms vary by activity and applicant, including individuals,FPOs and SHGs. No single cash payment for everyone. Use current official guidelines/application links; verify cost, contribution, bank and sanction terms.

Official source: https://dahd.gov.in/schemes/programmes/national_livestock_mission

### Livestock insurance - NLM component

Livestock insurance is an NLM component. Identification,species,local implementation,premium/subsidy,exclusions and claim deadlines depend on the current policy. Ask the veterinary officer for the insurer,policy document and tagging requirements. Reading about NLM does not start insurance cover.

Official source: https://dahd.gov.in/schemes/programmes/national_livestock_mission

### Sheep distribution - historical listing, intake unverified

Medak's 10 Jun 2019 listing describes sheep distribution for Yadava/Golla/Kuruma families. It does not establish current intake or subsidy rates. Confirm current intake,eligibility,contribution and availability with Animal Husbandry.

Official source: https://medak.telangana.gov.in/schemes/

### NSAP - National Social Assistance Programme

Central social assistance includes old-age, widow and disability pensions (IGNOAPS/IGNWPS/IGNDPS) and other components. Age, poverty criteria and state top-ups vary. Do not assume everyone gets an extra payment on top of a Telangana pension. Confirm enrolment, component, amount and current application route with the Gram Panchayat/MPDO.

Official source: https://nsap.nic.in/circular.do?method=faq

### PM Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

Central rural housing support for eligible households. The portal has progress and survey/verification notices. Survey entry, list verification, selection and sanction are separate steps. Ask the Gram Panchayat/MPDO about the current village list, objections and enrolment. Do not assume it is the same as Indiramma housing or that both benefits can be claimed.

Official source: https://pmayg.dord.gov.in/

### PM Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) - LPG connection

LPG connection scheme for eligible women from poor households. The official portal has eligibility, documents, benefits, distributor discovery and application routes. Confirm current rules including existing household LPG connections with the distributor. Connection assistance does not mean free refills forever. Refill subsidy/prices are separate from Telangana's Rs 500 cylinder component.

Official source: https://www.pmuy.gov.in/

### PM Surya Ghar - residential rooftop solar

The 29 Feb 2024 PIB release lists benchmark subsidy: Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW, capped at Rs 78,000 for 3 kW or more. Verify current rates, DISCOM approval, vendor, technical eligibility and final cost on the national portal. The 300-unit statement concerns estimated solar generation, not guaranteed free grid electricity. Any loan must be repaid.

Official source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?lang=2&PRID=2010133&reg=3
https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in

### PM-JANMAN - PVTG households and habitations

A multi-department mission for Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups(PVTGs):housing,water,sanitation,power,roads/telecom,health,education and livelihoods. Not a uniform cash scheme for all ST households. Confirm group/habitation coverage and service plans with ITDA/Tribal Welfare.

Official source: https://adiprasaran.tribal.gov.in/pm-janman/

### Kanti Velugu - confirm current eye camp

Medak's 3 Sep 2019 listing describes eye screening and free spectacles for those needing correction. It does not establish that a camp runs today. Confirm current local date, venue and services with the health department/PHC.

Official source: https://medak.telangana.gov.in/schemes/

### MCH Kit - current implementation unverified

CHFW lists MCH Kit for pregnant women/newborns. The listing does not establish current distribution, personal eligibility or cash amounts. Ask the PHC/ASHA for current orders, documents and availability. Separate from PMMVY cash benefits.

Official source: https://chfw.telangana.gov.in/programmes.html

### Indira Mahila Shakti - SHG livelihoods

SERP policy describes SHG micro-enterprise, skills, branding/packaging, marketing and processing-centre support. State bank-linkage targets are not individual cash entitlements. Confirm the applicable loan/subvention/training, eligibility and repayment with mandal SERP/bank.

Official source: https://efms.serp.telangana.gov.in/Livelihoods/TRIGP/MandalViews/HomeMHS.aspx

### ADIP - assistive devices for persons with disabilities

The official page, noting a 26 Sep 2024 revision, requires UDID card/enrolment, at least 40% disability certification and Aadhaar/enrolment. It lists 100% subsidy for monthly family income up to Rs 22,500; 50% for Rs 22,501-30,000; aids up to Rs 15,000 once in 3 years; high-end prostheses up to Rs 30,000; motorised tricycles/wheelchairs up to Rs 50,000 once in 5 years. Not cash for everyone: device/need conditions apply. Confirm current documents/approval through an implementing agency, PMD-VK or official ARJUN route.

Official source: https://depwd.gov.in/en/adip-scheme/

### SC Corporation - land purchase/distribution

The TSCCDCL portal describes support for landless poor Dalit agriculture-dependent families; last update 18 May 2024. Its visible Apply Online is for land SELLERS, not a verified beneficiary application. Confirm current intake,eligibility and beneficiary selection/enrolment with SC Corporation/MPDO.

Official source: https://lplds.cgg.gov.in/

### ST Corporation - TRICOR livelihood route

The state Tribal Welfare directory lists the ST Cooperative Finance Corporation. Confirm current livelihood/economic-support notices,eligibility,project,bank/contribution terms with ITDA/Tribal Welfare. A directory listing does not establish open applications.

Official source: https://www.telangana.gov.in/departments/tribal-welfare/

### BC Corporation / federation economic support

The official district page describes self-employment,bank-linked subsidy and occupational-group schemes. Do not treat older rates as current limits. Enrol under the current notification; ask BC Welfare/MPDO for current eligibility,documents and OBMMS route.

Official source: https://bhoopalapally.telangana.gov.in/b-c-development/bc-development/

### Dalit Bandhu - current intake unverified

The official portal describes SC-family livelihood support. Older sanctions/statistics and a 2024 update are not a new intake notice. Current selection,funding and village intake are unverified. Ask SC Corporation/MPDO for current orders/eligibility. Do not pay a middleman for promised sanction.

Official source: https://dalitbandhu.telangana.gov.in/

### TASK / SoFTNET - skills and education routes

TASK is the state skills/employability organisation,with training for registered students. Not every course is free/open for everyone. Medak's 2019 index lists SoFTNET/MANA TV educational broadcasting. Confirm current courses,fees,registration/broadcast details officially; no job guarantee.

Official source: https://task.telangana.gov.in/
https://medak.telangana.gov.in/schemes/

## Interpretation and release checks

- Land portal Apply Online is for land sellers, not verified beneficiary enrolment.
- Vidya Bharosa listing is not proof either of implementation or non-implementation.
- PMFME rates are historical; continuation/intake after March 2026 is unverified. Internship current terms are not inferred from older stipend notices.
- GVY equipment contribution is not a cash entitlement; current intake remains unverified. Rajiv Yuva Vikasam current intake remains unverified.
- PM Surya Ghar benchmark rates are dated 29 February 2024. Its portal could not be read during this check; current rate/vendor/DISCOM checks are required before action.
- Mahalakshmi component eligibility is separate; Rs2,500 payment status is unverified.
- Rythu Bharosa explanation: https://mulugu.telangana.gov.in/scheme/rythu-bharosa/ . Tenant/ROFR/landless-support details are attributed to that page and subject to individual verification.
- Existing cards not amended in this release retain their prior source dates; the global content date is not reset to imply every card was rechecked.
- Tested all 50 rows and each target in Telugu/English, 433 search checks, 22 new natural UI queries, narrow widths, unique IDs and preserved prior card IDs. v93 to v94 upgrade preserves progress and sibling caches; offline search verified.
